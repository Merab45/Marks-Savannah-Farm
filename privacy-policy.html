Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mark’s Savannah Farm website and Android app. It is written for the current version of the app, which is a prototype. The app’s account features currently store information in your browser on your device rather than in a farm-operated online account database.

1. Information you provide

If you create an app account, the prototype asks for your name, email address, and phone number. It also processes the password you choose to verify sign-in. The password itself is not saved as readable text; the browser stores a salted password hash for the local prototype account. If you contact the farm, the information you include in your call, email, or WhatsApp message is handled through that channel.

2. Where information is stored

Prototype account details and the sign-in session are saved in local browser storage on the device you use. They are not currently sent to a farm account server by the app. Browser or device access can expose locally stored information to someone who can use your device or browser profile. Clearing browser or app storage may erase your local account and session.

The site may cache app and website files on your device so pages can load more reliably, including when connectivity is limited. This cache contains site resources; it is separate from your account details.

3. How information is used

Information entered into the local account prototype is used to create and sign in to that local account. Information you send to the farm is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, or discuss a purchase or service. We do not describe the current prototype as providing online account synchronisation, Google authentication, or automated password recovery; those features are not connected yet.

4. Third-party services

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, that interaction is handled by WhatsApp and is subject to its own privacy policy and terms. Links to external services may take you away from this site. The app’s Google sign-in option is not currently connected to Google authentication.

5. Your choices and requests

You can remove the prototype account from a device by clearing this site’s browser or app storage, though that also removes other saved app information on that device. For a question or request about personal information you sent directly to the farm, email meraprenish@gmail.com or call +254 706 894 568. Please tell us enough to identify the enquiry without sending your password.

Kenya’s Data Protection Act provides data-subject rights, which may include being informed about the use of personal data, access, objection, correction, and deletion, subject to the law. You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya. See the ODPC’s data subject rights information.

6. Retention and security

Local prototype account data remains in browser storage until it is cleared or the browser or device removes it. Information you send to us may be retained as needed to respond to you and handle related business records. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; protect access to your device and do not reuse a password you use for an important account.

7. Children

The account features are intended for people able to make their own account decisions. A parent or guardian should contact us if a child has sent personal information to the farm.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our information practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Mark’s Savannah Farm at meraprenish@gmail.com or +254 706 894 568.
