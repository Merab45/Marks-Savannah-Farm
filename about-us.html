Our Story

Mark’s Savannah Farm

Mark’s Savannah Farm began in 1997 with a commitment to making agriculture a lasting part of family and community life. We serve customers across Kenya from Laikipia, Narok and Nakuru, bringing livestock, crops, poultry, dairy products, farm equipment and agri-consultancy together so every conversation starts with what you actually need.

  • Practical, grounded in real farming needs
  • Personal, direct and helpful conversations
  • Growing across Laikipia, Narok and Nakuru
